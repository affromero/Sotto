# Sotto — Logo Brief

> A focused brief for designing the Sotto logo (mark + wordmark). For the wider
> identity (color, type, UI) see `design-brief.md`.

## What Sotto is (so the mark means something)
Sotto is open-source language learning where you connect **your own AI agent** —
the one that already knows your work and interests — and it teaches you a language
in your own context. You own the agent, the data, the whole stack.

The name is Italian: **_sotto voce_ — "in a low, quiet voice."** A tutor speaking
just to you. That intimacy is the heart of the mark.

## The one job of the logo
Evoke **a quiet, personal, intelligent voice that is yours.** Intimacy + ownership.
Not "tech product," not "school," not "AI magic" — a calm, crafted mark that feels
like something you'd want to own. If it whispers, it's working.

Feeling words: **quiet · personal · crafted · owned · grown-up.**

## Concept territories to explore (pick/blend, don't do all)
1. **Sotto voce — the quiet line.** A spoken voice rendered as a single, soft,
   confident stroke: a soundwave calmed down to one gentle line or breath, not a
   loud equalizer or speech bubble. The mark *is* a quiet voice.
2. **Personal constellation (the memory graph).** Sotto's signature is your
   personal word/idea graph. A mark of a few elegantly connected nodes — your own
   knowledge as a small constellation. The two **o**'s in "Sotto" could *be* nodes,
   linked.
3. **The accent / diacritic.** Pronunciation's visual language is the accent mark.
   An accent, breve, or macron sitting over the wordmark — a "spoken" cue that's
   also a quiet flourish. Plays naturally with the double **t** / double **o**.
4. **Typographic intimacy.** A purely typographic wordmark where the craft is in
   the letterforms themselves (a custom **tt** ligature, the paired **oo**), no
   separate symbol — confidence through restraint.

## Wordmark
- Likely **lowercase** "sotto" (quieter, more intimate than all-caps) — but show a
  cap option.
- Custom or refined-typeset; it should feel literary and warm, not techy/geometric.
- Must read perfectly small and in one color.

## Mark / icon
- A standalone symbol (ideally derived from the wordmark — e.g. the linked **o**'s,
  the accent, or the quiet line) that stands alone as an **app icon + favicon**.
- Must survive at **16px**, in pure monochrome, knocked-out on both light (cream)
  and dark backgrounds.
- Calm at small sizes — no fine detail that muddies.

## Do
- Restraint, warmth, craft. One idea executed beautifully.
- Works in a single ink; works reversed on dark.
- Feels personal and owned, like a maker's mark.

## Don't
- ❌ Globes, flags, world maps (it's not "international").
- ❌ Speech bubbles, microphones, sound-equalizer bars (too literal/loud).
- ❌ Graduation caps, books, pencils, owls (edtech clichés).
- ❌ Robots, sparkles ✨, circuit lines, "AI" gradients.
- ❌ Gamified/cartoon/mascot energy.

## References for *spirit* (not to copy)
The restraint of Linear, the warmth of a literary/reading brand, the personal calm
of Obsidian's graph. Quiet confidence over decoration.

## Deliverables
- Primary lockup (mark + wordmark), plus wordmark-only and mark-only.
- App icon + favicon (16/32/180/512px).
- Monochrome (black, white) and 1-color-on-dark variants.
- Clear-space + minimum-size rules.
- Vector source (SVG/AI) + exported PNGs.

## Open questions for you ↔ designer
1. Keep the name **"Sotto"**? (Recommended — _sotto voce_ is the concept.)
2. Lean **warm/literary** or **sharp/tool-like**? (Suggest warm-but-restrained.)
3. **Symbol + wordmark**, or **wordmark-only**? (Wordmark-only is very on-brand for
   "quiet," but a tiny mark helps the app icon.)
