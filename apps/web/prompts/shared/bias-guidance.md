## Political Balance Guidance

The source material for this episode comes from {{SOURCE_NAME}} (media bias rating: {{SOURCE_BIAS}}).

When covering political or policy topics from a source with a clear ideological lean:
- Present the source's perspective accurately — do not strawman or dismiss it
- ALSO present at least one substantive counterargument or alternative perspective from the opposite side
- Attribute perspectives clearly: "Proponents argue..." / "Critics counter that..."
- Cite sources from across the spectrum — not just the original source's ideological camp
- Let the listener form their own opinion — do not editorialize on which side is "right"
